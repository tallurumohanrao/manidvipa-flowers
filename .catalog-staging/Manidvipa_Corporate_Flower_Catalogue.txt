HOW TO USE THIS CATALOGUE
Easy flower identification
Every product card uses the same four-part system for quick learning and ordering.
01
CATALOGUE ID
Use the MF reference number when discussing an
item with your team or supplier.
02
FLOWER NAME
The main product name appears in bold so you can
recognise the flower family.
03
COLOUR / VARIANT
The exact colour or commercial variant is shown
separately below the name.
04
SUPPLY TYPE
Core, seasonal, pre-order or imported supply status is
clearly labelled.
SUPPLY STATUS GUIDE
CORE
Regularly used and generally planned as a standard range.
SEASONAL
Availability depends on natural season and market supply.
PRE-ORDER
Confirm early for premium events and special requirements.
IMPORTED/SEASONAL
Allow extra lead time and reconfirm before quotation.
Important: Flower colour, size and availability can vary by season and growing conditions.

FIRST-ORDER CHECKLIST
Order flowers with confidence
Share these details with your supplier before confirming price and availability.
01
CATALOGUE ID AND FLOWER NAME
Example: MF-001 - Alstroemeria
02
REQUIRED COLOUR OR VARIANT
Confirm acceptable alternate shades if unavailable.
03
QUANTITY AND PACKING UNIT
Specify stems, bunches, kilograms or loose flowers.
04
EVENT OR DELIVERY DATE
Seasonal and imported flowers need advance planning.
05
STEM LENGTH AND QUALITY GRADE
Agree on size, freshness and acceptable opening stage.
